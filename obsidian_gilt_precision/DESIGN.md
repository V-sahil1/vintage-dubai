## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-exclusive digital showroom experience tailored for the high-net-worth automotive connoisseurs of Dubai and the UAE. It projects absolute authority, bespoke craftsmanship, and performance engineering under the ethos of personal distinction.

The aesthetic fuses **Architectural Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism** and high-precision mechanical detailing. The visual plane is anchored in deep, light-absorbing obsidian voids, allowing vehicle silhouettes, metallic reflections, and radiant champagne-gold illumination to define elevation and interactive tension. Generous, deliberate negative space communicates reserve, calm exclusivity, and museum-grade curation.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the twilight ambiance of an elite architectural gallery in Downtown Dubai:
- **Canvas & Backdrops:** Obsidian Black (`#0A0B0E`) serves as the true base ground, stepped gently to `#111317` for deep sectional shifts.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Rich Charcoal (`#181B20`) forms primary interactive planes, encased with structural borders (`#2A2F38`) that mimic high-grade machined titanium.
- **Accents:** Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) commands action and status, highlighted by Radiant Gold (`#E5C368`) and Pale Gilt (`#F3E5AB`) for hover reflections, backlit seams, and bespoke badges.
- **Typography & Details:** Pristine White (`#FFFFFF`) ensures critical legibility, balanced by Metallic Silver (`#E2E8F0`) and Liquid Platinum (`#94A3B8`) for secondary body details, dividing lines, and chassis spec labels.

## Typography

The type system blends avant-garde luxury geometry with instrumentation precision:
- **Display & Headlines (Syne):** Sculptural, wide-stance forms capture the commanding posture of bespoke coachbuilding. Display weights carry negative tracking to impart an editorial, monolithic presence.
- **Body & Editorial (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Pure, neutral, and ergonomically balanced, offering effortless readability over dark backdrops without distracting from vehicle photography.
- **Telemetry & Technical Specs (Space Mono):** Used for engine statistics (0–100 km/h, horsepower, torque), VIN disclosures, pricing tiers, and serial indices, recalling cockpit head-up displays and high-precision chronograph bezels.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column fluid grid system with structural restraint defines the viewport. Generous margins create an open runway around vehicle assets:
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, 64px (`4rem`) outer margins, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Content stays framed within a maximum container width of 1600px to honor panoramic showcase standards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 32px (`2rem`) margins, 20px gutters. Spec sheets collapse to dual-column telemetry tables.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 20px (`1.25rem`) margins, 16px (`1rem`) gutters. Gallery interactions pivot to seamless horizontal snap-scroll carousels, keeping hero vehicles edge-to-edge.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy operates through luminous layering rather than heavy drop shadows:
- **Base Canvas:** Flat `#0A0B0E` void.
- **Card Surfaces:** Semi-translucent `#181B20` mixed with 75% opacity and a `16px` backdrop-blur filter to evoke smoked museum glass.
- **Boundaries & Trim:** Structural 1px hairline borders tinted in `#2A2F38`. Active and focus states upgrade borders to champagne gold (`#D4AF37`) at 60% opacity with an ambient outer bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`).
- **Overlays & Modals:** Deep floating obsidian panels layered over a 90% opacity, 24px blurred canvas mask to keep attention completely focused on the vehicle or consultation scheduler.

## Shapes

The design system enforces architectural sharpness with subtle soft radii (`0.25rem` / `4px`). This prevents sterile harshness while preserving the razor-honed, precision-cut carbon-fiber aesthetic expected in hypercar engineering. Chips, technical tags, and micro-badges use strict rectangular geometry with 2px corner breaks.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary (Gold Halo):* Solid `#D4AF37` fill with dark `#0A0B0E` Syne text, or obsidian core wrapped in a 1px `#D4AF37` border backed by a soft radial gold aura.
  - *Secondary (Machined Titanium):* Background `#181B20`, border `#2A2F38`, pristine white text with hover transitions to liquid silver (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Vehicle Spec & Showcase Cards:** Smoked charcoal glass layers with a fixed 1px border. Hovering initiates a subtle zoom on the vehicle image and illuminates the border from `#2A2F38` to `#D4AF37`.
- **Telemetry Chips:** Rectangular badges utilizing Space Mono, framed in 1px `#2A2F38`, displaying transmission, power output, and acceleration figures.
- **Inputs & Inquiries:** Deep `#111317` input beds with recessed 1px borders, shifting to glowing champagne borders with white text upon activation.
- **Bespoke Inclusions:**
  - *Price & Currency Switcher:* Monospaced display featuring AED, USD, EUR, and GBP with realtime conversion indicators.
  - *VIP Concierge Trigger:* A floating persistent gold micro-trigger providing immediate WhatsApp and direct private broker access.
